### Baseline Defaults (Section 3 - Assumptions A1 to A16)
- **2026-10-17 | Working week definition (A1) |** Monday to Saturday are working days; Sunday does not require a site report.
- **2026-10-17 | Timezone standard (A2) |** Asia/Kolkata (IST) standard for all date logic and display. Database stores timestamps as `timestamptz`.
- **2026-10-17 | Currency & precision (A3 & Rule 1.4) |** Currency is INR formatted `en-IN`. All calculation amounts stored as integer paise in TypeScript, `numeric(14,2)` in Postgres. Floating-point arithmetic strictly forbidden.
- **2026-10-17 | UI language (A4) |** English only.
- **2026-10-17 | Project code sequence (A5) |** Format `WB-YYYY-NNN` generated by database sequence/function, unique.
- **2026-10-17 | Financial year period (A6) |** Indian Financial Year (April 1 to March 31). Presets include "This FY" and "Last FY".
- **2026-10-17 | System roles (A7) |** 6 roles: `director`, `project_manager`, `sales`, `purchase`, `site_engineer`, `accounts`. Multi-role users take union of permissions; director route wins on redirect.
- **2026-10-17 | Infrastructure targets (A8) |** Next.js App Router on Vercel; Supabase for Postgres, Auth, and Storage.
- **2026-10-17 | Fixed stage pipeline (A9) |** 9 stages fixed and seeded per Section 6. Editing stages is deferred.
- **2026-10-17 | Migration/Import policy (A10) |** No automated import of legacy Excel/manual data. Real records entered cleanly.
- **2026-10-17 | Amount & Tax convention (A11) |** Budgets, contracts, variations, orders, costs, and margins are strictly ex-GST. Invoices, bills, receipts, payments, and bank balances are cash numbers including GST. No statutory tax calculations.
- **2026-10-17 | TDS recording policy (A12) |** TDS is recorded as an input field on receipts and bill payments, never auto-computed.
- **2026-10-17 | Cost categories (A13) |** Initial 11 default categories seeded as active, manageable by director in Settings.
- **2026-10-17 | Sales meeting definition (A14) |** Follow-ups of kind `meeting` or `site_visit` count as attended meetings.
- **2026-10-17 | Bank balances source (A15) |** Manual dated entries only; no live bank APIs.
- **2026-10-17 | Lead sources and lost reasons (A16) |** Seeded from standard PEB defaults, editable by director.
- **2026-10-18 | Minimal Root Landing & Obscured Admin Route |** User requested public landing page (`/`) display solely "Westeel Control Dashboard" without visible portal option cards. Admin portal accessed via direct URL `/x9admin` (rewritten to `/admin`). Employee portal accessed via direct URL `/employee`. Navigation links on public screens removed.
- **2026-10-18 | Employee Access: Role Passcode / PIN Entry |** Kalki selected role-based passcode entry for `/employee`. Entering the role's designated password directly unlocks that specific role's interface, displaying only General items + that role's individual work (Site Engineer, Sales, Accounts, or Purchase & PM), filtering out all other roles.

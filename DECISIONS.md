- **2026-10-17 | Currency & precision (A3 & Rule 1.4) |** Currency is INR formatted `en-IN`. All calculation amounts stored as integer paise in TypeScript, `numeric(14,2)` in Postgres. Floating-point arithmetic strictly forbidden.

- **2026-10-17 | Cost categories (A13) |** Initial 11 default categories seeded as active, manageable by director in Settings.
- **2026-10-17 | Sales meeting definition (A14) |** Follow-ups of kind `meeting` or `site_visit` count as attended meetings.

Rule F13 financial immutability is implemented by disallowing invoice deletion and using soft cancellation (`is_cancelled = true`), which excludes cancelled invoices from aging, balances, and reports. Block 2 Receivables panel added to Director Overview at `/x9admin`.

Rules F6 (six cost states: Budget, Committed, Billed, Paid, Settled, Collected), F7 (payables aging & TDS settlement against bills), F8 (category cost forecast: billed + remaining committed + uncommitted buffer, verified against all 4 worked examples in brief), F9 (forecast profit & margin % with Incomplete badges when missing contract value or budget lines), F10 (cost overrun detection), and R9 (late vendor orders past expected delivery date) are computed live on read.
